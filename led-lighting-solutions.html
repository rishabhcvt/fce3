LED Lighting Solutions

LED has changed the way films are lit, and FCE3 is the proud pioneer of that change. We stock a wide range of LED panels, tubes, and fixtures from trusted manufacturers used across the industry. They're energy-efficient, they run cooler on set, and they give cinematographers a lot more control over colour temperature and intensity.

Our equipment is compatible with standard dimming systems, so integration into your existing setup is straightforward. We've supplied LED rigs for song sequences, night schedules, intimate dialogue scenes, the full range of what any production demands.

Our team can advise on which fixtures will work best for a given look if you need that input. The goal is always to get you what you need without overcomplicating things.
